# Administrator response guidance

Use this guidance for contact enquiries, booking questions, payment follow-up, and operational messages. Clinical advice must be handled by an assigned therapist, not by an administrator.

## Response targets

| Priority               | Examples                                                                       | First action                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Urgent                 | Immediate safety concern, self-harm language, abuse, or medical emergency      | Do not attempt counselling. Share the emergency-support route and direct the person to local emergency services immediately. Escalate internally to the clinical lead. |
| Same business day      | Failed payment, missing receipt, appointment within 48 hours, failed Meet link | Acknowledge, verify the booking reference, assign an owner, and resolve or escalate before close of business.                                                          |
| Within one working day | General service, pricing, therapist, availability, or policy enquiry           | Acknowledge, answer from approved site information, and record the outcome.                                                                                            |

## Required workflow

1. Open the enquiry in the admin and confirm its source, timestamp, and delivery state.
2. Check for crisis or safeguarding language before handling routine questions.
3. Verify identity before sharing booking, payment, form, or appointment details. Use the minimum information needed.
4. Reply through the approved Talk Space channel. Do not copy intake details or clinical notes into WhatsApp or ordinary email.
5. Assign unresolved payment, booking, notification, or Meet issues to the appropriate operational queue.
6. Record the action, owner, reason, and outcome in the admin where the workflow supports it.
7. Escalate clinical, safeguarding, refund-policy exceptions, or unresolved technical failures to the responsible lead.

## Channel rules

- The admin database is the source of truth for enquiries and delivery status.
- Zoho receives operational contact notifications; deleting an email must not be treated as deleting the admin record.
- WhatsApp may be used for brief scheduling coordination. Do not request diagnoses, clinical histories, assessment answers, payment secrets, or identity documents there.
- Never send passwords, API keys, access tokens, full card details, or private storage links.

## Before closing an enquiry

- Confirm the person received a useful next step.
- Confirm any promised booking, payment, form, or Meet action is reflected in the admin.
- Leave unresolved failures in their operational queue and assign/escalate them.
- Follow the approved retention policy; do not delete records merely to clear a queue.
